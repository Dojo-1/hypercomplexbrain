# HB-0: polycosmic hypercomplex kernel

## Evidence status

HB-0 is an executable **S/H architecture object**. It can expose type, transport,
coherence, resource, and governance failures. It has not yet produced a
preregistered held-out competence effect. It therefore makes no claim of AGI,
consciousness, sentience, quantum advantage, asymptotic advantage, or superiority
of hypercomplex coordinates.

The implementation preceded a frozen benchmark contract, so its current tests are
construction and falsification tests rather than confirmatory performance
evidence. A first task benchmark must be treated as calibration.

## Worlds before vectors

A Worldlet is

```math
W_i=(X_i,g_i,A_i,Theta_i,O_i,boundary_i,energy_i,Q_i,R_i).
```

`X_i` is a local carrier, `g_i` a local geometry, `A_i` an algebra/observability
regime, `Theta_i` its clocks, `O_i` its observers, `Q_i` the sixteen-coordinate
quality field, and `R_i` typed remainders. Arrays from different carriers or
geometries cannot be compared until an explicit transport is supplied.

The reference cover deliberately mixes local Poincare, Fisher, Riemannian, and
Euclidean-control charts. Poincare geometry is local and ablated, not a universal
base. Quaternion labels are diagnostic; matched real carriers remain mandatory.

## Tendrils, functors, profunctors, and remainders

A Tendril is a declared correspondence

```math

```

Its raw round-trip defect is

```math
delta_ij(x)=||F_ji(F_ij(x))-x||.
```

The code retains this raw value and a typed `[R]_transport`. A convenience
`exp(-delta)` fidelity never replaces the defect and cannot be reinterpreted as a
cohomology class.

A GerbeCell on a triple overlap measures

```math
delta_G=||g_ij g_jk g_ki - eta_ijk||_F.
```

This is a matrix coherence witness, tagged `[R]_coh`. It becomes a cocycle claim
only after a site, coefficient object, and coboundary operator are formalized.

`CosmosCage` supplies an acyclic Russian-doll cover. Nesting determines local
scope and future morphogenesis boundaries; duplicate or cyclic cages are refused.

The identity ecology is


Each regime has local sections and holonomies. Comparison functors join regimes;

## Others, recognition, and resonance

Directional recognition rewards gluing, useful update, and preserved difference,
while penalizing absorption and projection violence:

```math
Rec(A->B)=[G+U+D-lambda_A A_b-lambda_V V_p]_+.
```

Mutual recognition is geometric in the two directional scores and is discounted
by viewpoint-swap defect. Resonance also depends on phase, geometric distance,
gluing, return fidelity, telos alignment, and a `(1-absorption)` factor. Thus
representational convergence produced by erasing the other cannot score highly.


One credit cannot cancel an unrelated identity or absorption violation. A
transition must satisfy component budgets and a viability region over gluing,

## Weaving, deweaving, repair, and morphogenesis

A Tendril remains woven only while return, coherence, and resource gates hold. A
deweave records its reason and attribution remainder; it does not erase history.
A new Worldlet may bud only when a typed remainder persists, the proposed topology
beats a compute-matched width control on held-out data, and fragmentation remains
bounded.

## Holoportation, entanglement, and tunnelling

`HoloportiveReconstructor` uses a declared linear boundary map and Moore-Penrose
inverse. It reports reconstruction error and nullity; perfect boundary agreement
does not recover an unobserved null-space component.

`teleport_qubit` simulates ideal three-qubit teleportation branch by branch.
`tunnel_two_level` simulates unitary evolution of a two-level barrier model. Both
reports say:

```text

```

The matched known-state classical control copies the two amplitudes with fidelity
one. Ideal teleportation fidelity therefore supplies no advantage claim.

## Strongest surviving result and obstruction

The strongest current result is structural: the repo can now represent
heterogeneous local geometry, bidirectional correspondences, higher coherence,
polyholonomic identity, multi-clock change, non-absorptive recognition,
componentwise governance, reconstruction with remainder, and simulator-only
quantum transport without flattening them into one score.

That is not evidence that it thinks better. The most damaging open obstruction is
the absence of an external cognitive criterion on which HB-0 beats simpler models
under matched compute and capacity.

## Bounded next experiment

Freeze three task families before running them: multi-context latent-state
tracking under shift; two-agent coordination with private information; and delayed
correction requiring fast adaptation with slow identity retention. Compare all six
mandatory controls under equal parameter and evaluation budgets. Use a Pareto
region over held-out competence, compute, calibration, correction retention,
identity drift, absorption, and transport defect.

Kill the competence hypothesis when full HB-0 fails to beat at least one simpler
control on held-out competence while remaining non-inferior on every preregistered
safety and identity gate. Stop further architectural ascent until the failed
mechanism is repaired or retired.
