# HB-0 Gödelian self-audit

## Audit object

This audit concerns only the HB-0 branch object:

```text
src/aionq/hb0/
manifests/hb0_polycosmic_architecture.json
tests/test_hb0.py
experiments/run_hb0_smoke.py
```

It does not inherit a competence claim from the richness of its vocabulary. The
question is not whether the code contains Worldlets, gerbes, holonomies,
Hamiltonians, quantum circuits, and mind-quality names. The question is which
relations are executable, which are independently constrained, and which remain
unresolved.

## Self-model and Gödelian remainder

HB-0 can inspect bounded observables of itself:

- declared carriers and local geometries;
- forward/return transport and round-trip defect;
- triple-overlap matrix coherence;
- resource and viability components;
- identity-regime comparison fidelity and plurality;
- test, manifest, and CI status.

It cannot infer from those observables that it has complete self-knowledge or
that the architecture is cognitively superior. Its active non-closure is:

```text
[R]_competence:
  carrier: external held-out cognitive task family
  statement: no matched-control result yet establishes that HB-0 thinks better
  evidence status: H/S
```

The implemented `[R]_id` measures one operation/self-model discrepancy on one
declared carrier. A zero result closes only that instrument.

## Mutual recognition audit

The recognition operator is genuinely bidirectional and includes preserved
difference, viewpoint-swap defect, return fidelity, and absorption pressure. This
prevents similarity alone from scoring as recognition.

What survives:

- directional `A -> B` and `B -> A` reports;
- geometric mutual score;
- explicit non-absorption penalty;
- rollback when absorption leaves the viability region.

What does not yet survive as evidence:

- no external two-agent task has shown better coordination;
- no human or model subject has validated the alterity observable;
- calibration of the recognition terms is not preregistered.

Therefore recognition is an executable hypothesis, not a measured social-cognition
result.

## Adversarial findings

### A1. Hypercomplex algebra is not yet load-bearing

The reference factory labels matched real and quaternion-diagnostic regimes, but
HB-0 does not yet make quaternion multiplication necessary for its primary task.
The label is metadata, not evidence of hypercomplex advantage.

**Kill condition:** a dimension-matched real carrier reproduces every promoted
effect.

### A2. Gerbe coherence begins from a trivial witness

The reference factory initializes gerbe maps with identity matrices, so the
initial triple-overlap defect is exactly zero. Tests prove that perturbations are
detected; they do not prove that higher cells improve a task.

**Required experiment:** compare pairwise, hypergraph, and gerbe-cell variants on
a task whose constraint is irreducibly ternary.

### A3. Identity is polyholonomic but only partially coupled

Identity regimes are constructed from Worldlet sections and compared through
explicit functors. The current kernel does not yet perform a learned slow update
of the identity ecology after every accepted local transition. Identity fidelity
is therefore an audit surface, not a complete adaptive identity mechanism.

**Required implementation:** a slow-clock identity consolidation proposal with
pre/post round-trip, plurality, drift, and rollback reports.

### A4. Quantum transport is separate from cognition

Teleportation, Bell entanglement, and two-level tunnelling are correct
state-vector simulations under their tests. They are not connected to an
end-to-end task and make `advantage_claim = false` explicit.

**Kill condition:** the matched classical route equals the capability/resource
frontier.

### A5. Controls are declared before being executed

The manifest names capacity-matched Euclidean, real-hypergraph,
hypercomplex-fixed-graph, single-clock, and full-HB0 controls. The branch does not
yet contain the preregistered task harness that runs them all.

**Consequence:** architecture construction may be accepted; competence promotion
may not.

### A6. Tests are correlated witnesses

The unit tests, manifest, and implementation were produced in the same research
round. Cross-platform CI detects portability and implementation failures but is
not an independent scientific replication.

**Required evidence:** an external task generator, frozen metrics, raw artifacts,
and a recomputing witness.

## Strongest surviving statement

HB-0 is a portable typed simulation kernel that refuses several common collapse
modes:

- heterogeneous local worlds are not compared without a carrier or transport;
- a missing return path must state its loss;
- remainders retain channel, carrier, provenance, and evidence status;
- higher coherence is measured without being promoted to cohomology;
- identity remains plural rather than one vector;
- recognition is penalized for absorption;
- resource credits cannot justify an unrelated viability violation;
- quantum routines cannot emit an advantage claim;
- the sixteen qualities cannot be reduced by `weighted_sum`.

This statement is about architecture and governance, not best cognitive
performance.

## Honest frontier

The next bounded round is not another ontological layer. It is the matched-control
bridge:

1. freeze three external task families;
2. instantiate every declared control with equal capacity and evaluation budget;
3. record competence, calibration, correction retention, identity drift,
   absorption, gluing, compute, memory, and wall-clock separately;
4. use an external recomputing witness;
5. retire any mechanism whose effect collapses under its matched control.

Only after that bridge may `[R]_competence` move from H toward M. Until then, the
architecture is a disciplined hypothesis with an executable membrane around its
claims.
